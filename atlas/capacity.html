Water-demand assumptions

Purified feed 10 L/kg H₂, purification recovery 80%, and additional cooling intake 0 L/kg H₂ give 12.5 L/kg source intake. These are screening assumptions, not source-specific treatment validation. Cooling is excluded. The electricity default is the DOE historical PEM system benchmark.

Intake (m³/day) = MW × 24 × operating fraction × 12.5 ÷ electricity intensity. A lower operating factor reduces period-average demand; this does not model instantaneous withdrawal.

Only sites with fully supported water budgets appear here. Comparing up to four sites is optional. The atlas still shows the wider geographic inventory.

Evidence, calculation limits and detailed assessment

Public storage and annual-use records do not automatically establish unallocated dry-period water. The linked historical evidence and missing terms remain visible for each site. No margin is invented from lake area or stored volume.
