What this tells us

The assumed plant intake is smaller than this conditional annual residual. This supports further investigation, not a conclusion that water is available for the project. Reuse production is deducted as if fully consumed outside the treated-flow boundary; meter overlap must be checked. Seasonal flows, other commitments, protected discharge, water quality, delivery and permissions remain unresolved.

What is ready, and what remains open?

Ready for research review: linked public measurements, reproducible arithmetic, three-input demand calculation, alternative-source comparison and evidence exports. Not ready: dependable dry-period sizing, current uncommitted water allocation, or site approval.

Measurements describe 2024, not current operating conditions. No estimated confidence interval is claimed. The full source inventory has not been exhaustively reviewed.
