What this does—and what evidence is still needed

Source water intake per kilogram of hydrogen = purified feed ÷ recovery + additional cooling/utility intake. Hydrogen production follows electricity input and the selected electricity intensity.

Signed water budget = adjusted source supply − existing commitments − protected reserve. Margin = signed budget − plant intake. Negative margin is a shortfall; a negative budget shows an existing deficit before this plant. For the secondary screening threshold, remaining water is clamped to zero. Threshold capacity = chosen share × remaining water ÷ source intake per MW.

The calculation uses daily rates representing the supplied period. It does not model reservoir storage, aquifer recharge, sub-daily low flows, salinity, return-flow timing, habitat response or cumulative impacts. For wastewater, diversion may reduce downstream flows; account for that in the protected reserve.

For estuaries, seawater, wetlands, drains and other unsuitable or ambiguous features, a mapped object does not establish an abstractable source. The calculation requires a separately justified source budget.
