# Researcher review

This tool is a research prototype with historical annual scenarios, not a water permit or dry-period sizing assessment.

- Does the research question and plant-water boundary match your project?
- Can you reproduce the worked example from the cited operator page?
- Are treated-water and reuse measurements separate, or could reuse be counted within a circulation loop?
- Which seasonal flow, existing allocation or environmental requirement would most change the interpretation?
- Does the export contain the evidence needed for your research workflow?
- Would you use the tool in research, and for which specific task? Please distinguish potential use from use already undertaken.

Feedback can be shared with the author; this page sends no messages or personal data.
