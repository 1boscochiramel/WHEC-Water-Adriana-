# OpenStreetMap data attribution and licence

## Separate EEA treatment-plant layer

Treatment-plant locations are sourced from the European Environment Agency,
Waterbase / Urban Waste Water Treatment Directive, public TreatmentPlants_2022
service. Source acknowledgement is retained in the interface, feature popups,
export and source package. EEA reuse policy:
https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/legal-notice

Catalogue: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/6ffd0af1-64eb-4cce-9970-0028e01ee64e

This layer is independently downloadable as app/data/lowgrade.json and is not
relicensed as OSM data. Its point records and OSM polygons remain separate layers.

## OSM water geometry

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, made available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) by OpenStreetMap contributors. Extract supplied by the Humanitarian
OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) through the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX).

- Copyright and attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Dataset: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_prt_waterways

The derived atlas database (index and simplified geometry) is made available
under ODbL 1.0. The original snapshot and scripts for producing the derivative
are included in portugal-water-atlas-source.zip. Retain attribution and the
applicable ODbL notices when sharing or modifying the database. The application's
MIT licence does not relicense the data or Leaflet.
