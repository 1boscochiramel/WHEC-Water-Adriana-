What qualifies as a supported budget?

A source-specific net allowance in m³/day for a stated period, backed by traceable supply, commitments and protected-water evidence, or an authoritative net available allowance with an explicit scope. Design capacity, annual treatment, storage, reuse targets and announced projects alone do not qualify.
