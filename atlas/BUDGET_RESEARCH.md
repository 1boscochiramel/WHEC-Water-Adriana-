# Portugal water-budget evidence audit

Reviewed 14 September 2026. This release expands the register to **33 partial-evidence source records**: 32 wastewater facilities and the Alqueva reservoir record. **No fully supported net dry-period budget has been established.** The expansion audits 31 distinct EEA wastewater records against primary operator pages; Afonsoeiro and Alqueva retain their earlier reviews. These counts are checked by `verify_research_expansion.py` and `verify_support_register.py`.

## What the search establishes

The strongest new evidence is site-level annual treated-water throughput in Tejo Atlantico's facility directory. It provides a factual operating baseline, and many pages also describe reclaimed-water production and existing reuse. The operator's Torres Vedras notice goes further by identifying a licensed monthly reuse quantity for urban uses. Neither annual throughput nor an authorised quantity demonstrates the unused dry-period supply available to a new hydrogen project.

The Algarve directory describes treatment, internal reuse and receiving waters. Its July 2026 update identifies reuse-production subsystems and ongoing additions. The regional annual report distinguishes internal reuse, ecosystem support and external reuse, while the monthly wastewater table is grouped by municipality. Those aggregate figures cannot be assigned to a specific plant or treated as an uncommitted allowance.

The calculator therefore continues to show **insufficient data** for margins and feasibility rankings. That is a data gap, not a conclusion that these sites are unsuitable. All mapped records are retained.

| Evidence family | What was checked | Decision |

| Seasonal records | Algarve monthly wastewater PDF | Municipality-level aggregation prevents exact-plant assignment |
| Reuse delivery and permits | Torres Vedras licence notice, Beirolas Parque Tejo case study, Quinta do Lago contract notice, Obidos protocol | Licence, existing use and proposed use are different; no unused share inferred |
| Current project status | Algarve July 2026 update, Ericeira and Santa Cruz upgrade notices | Replace blanket planned-only framing where newer evidence exists; do not assume a scheduled completion occurred |
| Basin plans and permit leads | APA indexed basin-plan measures, targeted TURH/TUA and reuse searches | APA direct download timed out; search excerpts are leads, not verified complete permits |
| Historical research leads | Search located older SimTejo monthly-flow research and an Almargem university thesis | Not imported as current supply; primary operational series and updated allocation still needed |

Searches included Portuguese terms `caudais mensais`, `volume de agua residual tratada`, `reutilizacao`, `licenca`, `TUA`, `TURH`, `disponibilidade` and `tempo seco`, combined with plant/operator names. Directory coverage is strongest in Algarve and Tejo/Oeste. This is an expanded, bounded public-source audit, **not proof that every public archive or every Portuguese source has been exhausted**. The national unreviewed inventory remains visible.

## Best next evidence to obtain

1. **Torres Vedras:** full TUA and current conditions; actual monthly production and deliveries; committed urban demand; compatibility of the proposed use. The operator notice establishes an authorisation claim, not the full permit or spare water.
2. **Beirolas and other Tejo reuse plants:** current delivery schedules, dry-period production and uncommitted quantities. Annual production and existing deliveries can help frame a request but cannot replace those terms.
3. **Vilamoura, Quinta do Lago, Boavista, Albufeira Poente and VRSA:** per-plant operating series and signed reuse allocations, alongside any environmental delivery requirements. The July 2026 system-wide summary is not an allocation table.
4. **Almargem and Faro Noroeste:** confirm commissioning and operating data for the additional reuse works before estimating dependable delivery.
5. **Other annual-data plants:** site-specific monthly/daily records, applicable discharge obligations and evidence of connection/permission for the intended use.

These are priorities for evidence collection, not a ranking of hydrogen-site feasibility. An operator or researcher may be able to supply publicly releasable documents that an indexed web search does not expose. No outreach was sent.

## Reproducibility and limits

`data/research30/evidence.json` stores the exact matched EEA ID/name, match rationale, source URL, retrieval date, original-file SHA-256, a short source anchor, finding and missing terms. Raw operator HTML and downloaded reports are retained locally for audit; they are not republished as open-licensed original content. The source package includes the factual manifest and `fetch_research_evidence.py`, which downloads from original publishers and fails if bytes have changed since review. Dynamic source pages can change; a hash mismatch requires a new review, not silent acceptance.

Run `verify_operator_values.py` to reproduce the published annual treated-water values for Rio Maior, Torres Vedras and Zambujeira from the archived primary pages. Run `verify_research_expansion.py` for distinct IDs, hashes, anchors and budget-null checks. `verify_support_register.py` checks complete inventory preservation and stage totals. Additional-source checks are in `verify_research_supplements.py`.

These checks verify extraction, identity bookkeeping and evidence boundaries. They do not independently validate operator measurements, treatment performance, abstraction permissions, intake connectivity or future water availability. No operator or Adriana has yet validated this expanded register.
