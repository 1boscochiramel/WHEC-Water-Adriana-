KNOW WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

Public geography. Clear limits.

Water geometry: OpenStreetMap contributors, supplied through the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) Portugal waterways extract on HDX.

Wastewater facilities: EEA Waterbase / UWWTD treatment plants, PT_2022 reported records. Facility points and OSM areas are separate inventories; they are not deduplicated or connected. Industrial effluent, mine water, stormwater and brackish groundwater networks are not covered.

Dataset & original download ↗ · Attribution & ODbL licence ↗

  • Includes mapped features across mainland Portugal, the Azores and Madeira. Coverage is not exhaustive: small, seasonal or unmapped waters may be absent.
  • A feature can be a river segment, water polygon or water-related structure. Several features may describe the same water body.
  • Types come from source tags. Missing or ambiguous tags remain “Other water” or “Other mapped features”; “reservoir” does not imply a large dam.
  • Shapes are simplified for display. They are not cadastral boundaries or current water levels. Border waters may extend outside Portugal.
  • No groundwater inventory, water quality, supply availability, rights or strain assessment is included. Wastewater polygons do not identify verified reuse supplies.

Basemap tiles load from OpenStreetMap. Search and filters run locally; no account or API key is required. The bundled water snapshot remains viewable if the basemap is unavailable.
